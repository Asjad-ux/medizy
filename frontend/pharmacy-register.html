Medizy Pharmacy Registration – Terms & Conditions

Valid License Requirement

The pharmacy must possess a valid and active drug license issued by the appropriate authority.

Accurate Business Information

All submitted details, including pharmacy name, address, and contact information, must be true and up to date.

Google Maps Presence*

The pharmacy must be listed and publicly visible on Google Maps with correct location details.

Authenticity of Medicines

Only genuine, approved, and legally sourced medicines may be sold through the platform.

Prescription Compliance

Prescription-only medicines must not be dispensed without a valid prescription from a licensed medical practitioner.

Stock & Availability Updates

The pharmacy is responsible for maintaining accurate and real-time updates of medicine availability.

Right to Suspension or Termination

Medizy reserves the right to suspend or terminate any pharmacy account found violating these terms.

About Medizy

Smart Healthcare, Simplified

Medizy is an AI-powered healthcare platform designed to provide quick and reliable medicine information to users. Our goal is to make healthcare more accessible, smarter, and efficient.


Users can scan prescriptions, search medicines, compare prices, and interact with our AI assistant (MediBot) for guidance.


We aim to bridge the gap between patients and accurate medical information using modern technology.

📌 Features

• Prescription scanning
• Medicine search
• AI chatbot assistance
• Price comparison
• Analytics insights

🎯 Our Mission

To make healthcare information easily accessible to everyone using AI-driven solutions.

🚀 Vision

To become a trusted digital healthcare assistant for users worldwide.

💡 Technology

Built using modern web technologies and AI to deliver fast and accurate results.

About Medizy

Smart Healthcare, Simplified

🏥 What is Medizy?

Medizy is an AI-powered healthcare platform designed to provide quick and reliable medicine information to users. Our goal is to make healthcare more accessible, smarter, and efficient. Users can scan prescriptions, search medicines, compare prices, and interact with our AI assistant (MediBot) for guidance. We aim to bridge the gap between patients and accurate medical information using modern technology.

📌 Features

Prescription scanning and OCR analysis · Medicine image authentication · AI chatbot assistance (MediBot) · Real-time price comparison across stores · Nearby pharmacy search · Analytics insights dashboard

🎯 Our Mission

To make healthcare information easily accessible to everyone using AI-driven solutions. We believe every patient deserves fast, accurate, and trustworthy medicine information at their fingertips.

🚀 Our Vision

To become a trusted digital healthcare assistant for users worldwide — a single platform that connects patients, pharmacies, and intelligent health data seamlessly.

💡 Technology

Built using modern web technologies, AI/ML models, and real-time databases to deliver fast and accurate results. Powered by Node.js, Flask, and intelligent APIs working together.
